# Cell portfolio template

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Vite and Three.js; local-only, single-page application in an isolated folder. Existing workspace deletions and unrelated files are preserved.

## Users
Research and internship reviewers exploring a high-school student's work.

## Product Purpose
Present the student's projects most prominently, followed by experience, activities, academics, honors, and contact. All personal information is explicitly editable placeholder content.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Scroll-driven entry through an initially closed, opaque low-poly animal cell; repeated deconstruction and reconstruction with every organelle packed inside before shell closure; one permanent full-viewport canvas with continuously authored specimen travel between reading columns with solid cores and feathered edges; a lower-right capsule navigation matching the supplied Anime.js reference, with five separated tick groups, accessible category jumps, hover/focus names, and a coral marker; no top bar; smooth half-distance scrolling; three featured projects, expandable reading panel, simplified mobile 3D, static and reduced-motion fallbacks. The cell shrinks to a tiny specimen inside a Petri dish before Project 2's microscope magnification. Reduced motion bypasses scroll easing and spatial animation. No filler scroll prompts, backend, publishing, contact form, fabricated achievements, or imported personal facts.

## Brand Commitments
Anime.js homepage is the user's interaction and visual reference. Charcoal and Minecraft red sand #BF6721 define the primary world. Projects 1 and 3 have right-side text; Project 2 has left-side text and an electron-microscope sequence with a smooth transition to a pale silver-green palette and dark monochrome specimen after docking. The warm palette returns for Project 3. Experience pairs left-side text with folded networks, Academics pairs left-side text with right-side ribosomes, and Contact centers its invitation beneath the complete reassembled cell without covering its silhouette. Two solid membrane halves physically open and hold an anatomical cross-section before camera entry. Fine technical 3D linework, category-linked organelles, gentle ambient drift; static diagrams preserve the split and microscopy states for reduced motion and unavailable 3D.
