# Portfolio journey

Mode: Experience. Local-only, code-led implementation of the user-approved plan; procedural 3D is the visual asset, with no raster comp required by the approved scope.

## Direction contract
THESIS: One closed cell becomes a cinematic journey through its interior. Repeated disassembly, close inspection, and reconstruction replace a static exploded diagram; projects lead the journey.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal #1c1d1b, off-white #ecebe6, muted #b6b7af, sand orange #bf6721 and light copper #e0a06d. Graphik and Wotfard, low-poly geometry with planar lighting and sparse anatomical contours. One permanent full-viewport projection plane allows uninterrupted specimen travel; reading surfaces keep solid cores beneath the text and feather their edges into the scene. The sole navigation is a charcoal capsule at the lower right, matching the supplied Anime.js placement: five equal tick groups, clear gaps, accessible jumps, and a coral marker. Phones retain a usable full-width bottom capsule. Names appear only on hover or keyboard focus; no top bar, duplicate identity, numeric endpoints, or footer template note.

STORY: Begin outside an opaque cell. Its solid membrane halves pull apart and hold an anatomical cross-section before camera entry. The same cell travels continuously between chapters, without switching canvas position or size. Projects 1 and 3 have right-side text. For Project 2, the cell moves right, shrinks to a tiny specimen, and settles inside a glass Petri dish beneath the electron column. Hold the placement, then magnify into the specimen as the palette fades smoothly to pale silver-green. Its left desktop panel leaves the microscope visible. Project 3 restores warmth. Experience has left-side text and folded networks; Academics keeps its text left of the ribosomes and clear of the lower-right ruler; Contact centers the invitation below the complete reassembled cell, with a clear gap and no silhouette overlap. Open in two padded stages: clear the shell first, then unfold the interior. Every closure packs all organelles and split internal structures before the halves close. Each physical stage receives one ease directly from raw scroll progress; shell and contents share one scroll pose. Cushioned starts and stops, broader reconstruction intervals, and perspective-scaled camera travel give each reveal time to settle. Reverse scrolling retraces each path. Half-distance smooth scrolling remains; reduced motion uses static diagrams.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name and short introduction left, closed orange faceted cell right, five-category capsule at the lower right, with a clear top edge. No organelles or leader lines show at rest. Solid reading cores with feathered edges preserve legibility over the permanent projection plane. Phones frame the specimen above the copy. Each equal ruler segment tracks local progress through its category's actual document interval.

FORM: User-pinned Anime.js choreography and varied compositions, retaining the dark/sand palette and portfolio placeholders, with one explicit electron-microscope palette break in Project 2. Signature interactions are the physical half-shell opening, held cross-section, reversible camera entry, repeated structure disassembly/reassembly, and microscope docking.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
